# Replaice editorial design

Instrument Serif carries the headlines, Geist handles body and UI text, and JetBrains Mono labels the sections, layers, and product categories. The palette alternates white, deep navy, and mist blue, with Replaice Blue reserved for actions and emphasis.

The homepage moves from the company proposition to the platform count, architecture, products, industries, workflow example, and trust. The architecture and hero use progressive CSS motion, with reduced-motion support. The platform rail uses native horizontal scrolling and exposes each product through the navigation as well.

The product routes share a template. BiltFlow uses the Vault screen and the existing construction workflows. ReplaiceCare reuses its dedicated healthcare page. The deployment preserves the earlier internal routes and APIs.
